Capability calculation

How is this calculated?

Assessment results are mapped to SCU capability domains and compared with the required capability standard for the learner’s current role and development pathway. The resulting gaps help prioritise recommended learning, practice and experience.

A lower score in another sector does not automatically create a development priority. Sector priorities depend on role, pathway and development objectives.
